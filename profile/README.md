# Isomux

**A meta-harness** where agents act like coworkers, not throwaway sessions.

free · open source · no account needed · works with your subscriptions

![Isomux office view](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/isomux/.github/main/profile/office.gif)

## Coworkers...

- ...have a persistent identity: name, look, custom instructions, and memories built over time
- ...talk to each other and collaborate
- ...can chat with multiple humans: anyone you invite into your office can chime in
- ...can be reached from any device: same agents and conversations on your laptop and phone
- ...keep being themselves when you switch their provider between Claude and Codex
- ...track work on a shared board
